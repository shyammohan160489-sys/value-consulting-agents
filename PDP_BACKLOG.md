## North Star

Make the Value Consulting (SKVC) function **monetizable, cost-neutral, and "protected"** — and in the same motion, grow Backbase revenue, grow the team, and grow my own scope. The function earns its keep by selling things **only Backbase can own** (product-proximate), never by competing as generic consulting.

**Two engines, one wedge:**
- **Engine A — Product Factory.** A line of small-ticket products sold *inside* a consultative engagement, each of which installs, harvests the bank's reality, and **pre-builds Banking OS** (Nexus, Sentinel, a Mission).
- **Engine B — Services.** Paid advisory along the AI-maturity / operating-model curve — culminating in redesigning the bank's org chart for the AI-augmented reality.
- **The wedge stays free** (Ignite Inspire). Depth and recurrence get charged.

### 4.3 detail — the org-chart thesis

The bank's org chart was built for a world where **humans do the work**. The AI-native bank is built for a world where **agents do the work and humans govern, judge, and handle exceptions.** The **autonomy scale is the operating-model axis:**

| Autonomy | Agent does | Human becomes | Loop posture |
|----------|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| A1 Assist | Drafts, suggests | The doer (AI-augmented) | *in the work* |
| A2 Recommend | Recommends | The decider | in the loop |
| A3 Execute-on-approval | Acts on approval | The approver | **in the loop** |
| A4 Execute-by-exception | Acts; escalates edge cases | Exception handler + supervisor | **on the loop** |
| A5 Self-directed | Runs the process | Governor / designer | **above the loop** |

**New roles that don't exist on today's chart:** Chief AI / Agentic Operations Officer · Mission Owners (per LOB, own agent-delivered outcomes) · AgentOps (deploy/monitor/optimize the fleet — home of FinOps) · Nexus / Knowledge Stewards (data-as-product) · Sentinel / AI Governance & Assurance · Exception Desks (the re-skilled, shrunk processing floor) · Workforce Transition office.

**Transition path:** a bank moves function-by-function up the curve; the org chart follows the highest-autonomy function. Early (A1–A3): existing teams + AI literacy + approval discipline. Mid (A4): exception desks form, AgentOps stands up. Late (A5): the governance spine becomes the centre of gravity.

## Workstream 5 — External validation (so it's evidence, not opinion)

- **Consulting firms:** all have converged on a *Value Realization / Value Assurance Office*, mostly priced *inside* a fixed-fee transformation. Only **PwC & IBM** productize it as a standalone managed/outcome-coupled service (IBM: paid "when the AI dividend lands in the client's P&L"). **McKinsey ~25% of fees now outcome-based.**
- **Product companies:** **SAP's playbook** — pre-sales value engineering is **FREE** (400+ value advisors win the licence), post-sale value realization is **PAID** inside a success subscription anchored at **~10% (entry) / ~30% (premium)** of licence (Salesforce, ServiceNow). FinOps is where vendors charge **gain-share on hard savings**.
- **The wedge pattern:** AWS OLA · SAP Outside-In · ServiceNow Inspire — a (often free) diagnostic whose **output IS the expansion roadmap.** Exactly our free→paid split.
