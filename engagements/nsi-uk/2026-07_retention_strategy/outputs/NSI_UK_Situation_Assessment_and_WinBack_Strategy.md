## 1. Executive Summary

NS&I — a live Backbase customer via its adviser portal — decided under its previous leadership to replatform its digital front end onto a custom IBM-built stack as part of its Business Transformation Programme (formerly "Rainbow"). That programme is now in open, documented crisis: **£1.3bn over budget (£1.7bn → ~£3bn), roughly four years late, branded a "full-spectrum disaster" by Parliament's Public Accounts Committee, with no timetable for replacing the website and a CEO who resigned in 2026** over a separate bereavement-claims scandal. The programme is in formal "reset," and NS&I has been ordered by the PAC to produce a realistic, bottom-up integrated plan.

This creates a rare and time-bound window. The people who chose to build rather than buy are largely discredited or gone; a new CEO (Sir Jim Harra, ex-HMRC) must demonstrate value-for-money to the Treasury and the NAO; and every workstream in the programme will be re-justified during the re-plan. **Backbase's recommended play is not a sales campaign — it is a "de-risk the recovery" positioning:** repair the relationship first, keep the one thing that works (the Backbase adviser portal) flawless, and enter the re-plan conversation as the lowest-risk, fastest, most defensible option for the digital experience layer — ideally *with* IBM as the delivery partner rather than against them.

**Recommendation:** Execute the four-phase strategy in Section 5, beginning with a listening exercise (no pitch) within 30 days. Decision gate at 90 days: pursue partner-through-IBM route, or position for the re-procurement that a programme extension beyond March 2028 will likely force.

## 2. Who NS&I Is (and why they are unusual)

- **State savings bank, not a normal bank.** NS&I is an Executive Agency of HM Treasury with 24+ million customers, best known for Premium Bonds. Its funding is government debt; its mandate is balancing savers, taxpayers, and the gilt market.
- **Consequence for strategy:** decisions are governed by public procurement law, HM Treasury spending controls, NAO audit, and Parliamentary scrutiny (PAC). "Value for money" is not a slogan — it is the legal test their Accounting Officer must personally certify. Every argument we make must be framed in those terms, not vendor ROI terms.
- **Backbase footprint today:** the financial adviser portal, built on Backbase, is live and publicly credited with lifting adviser satisfaction from 58% to 78% — NS&I's best score since 2011. This is our proof point and our beachhead.

### What this means, read as a consultant

1. **The build-over-buy decision is publicly failing.** Two and a half years into the IBM Digital Experience contract, the NAO records *no timetable for replacing the website*. The custom replatform of the front end has not landed.
2. **The decision-makers who deselected Backbase are weakened or gone.** A new CEO from HMRC — an institution that itself learned hard lessons about mega-programmes — must now personally own a defensible plan.
3. **Every workstream will be re-justified.** The PAC-mandated bottom-up re-plan, plus the Treasury funding negotiation, means each tower (digital experience, contact centre, core) faces a fresh cost/risk/benefit test in 2026.
4. **Contracts create a 2028 forcing function.** IBM's digital contracts end Mar/Aug 2028; Atos ends Mar 2028. Any extension of the programme beyond 2028 (E9) likely requires new commercial cover — extension, variation, or re-procurement. That is our structural entry point.
5. **The bereavement scandal is the emotional centre of gravity.** 37,500 grieving families, £476m withheld, a CEO resignation. Any proposal that visibly fixes customer-facing pain — fast — has outsized political value.

## 5. Strategy: "De-Risk the Recovery"

**Positioning statement (internal north star):**
> *NS&I does not need another transformation promise. It needs the shortest, most defensible path to a working digital front end — one its Accounting Officer can certify to the NAO. Backbase is the only option already live, already measured, and already paid for at NS&I.*

Three strategic principles before the phases:

- **Never gloat, never reference the crisis.** NS&I's leadership is under brutal public scrutiny. The moment Backbase is seen exploiting their embarrassment, the door closes. All framing is forward-looking: "fastest safe path," never "we told you so."
- **Partner with IBM, don't fight them.** IBM holds contracted scope to Aug 2028 and shares blame diplomatically with NS&I in the NAO report ("incomplete understanding of dependencies" — E6). Head-on displacement of IBM is unwinnable and procedurally slow. The winning shape: **IBM keeps the SI/integration/hosting scope; Backbase becomes the product layer IBM delivers on.** IBM's delivery risk drops, NS&I's timeline shortens, Backbase is reinstated. Everyone exits the crisis with a win.
- **Procurement route determines everything.** NS&I cannot simply "choose Backbase back." Viable routes, in order of speed: (a) product substitution *within* IBM's existing contract via change control; (b) call-off under an existing framework (G-Cloud / DOS); (c) participation in the re-procurement an extension beyond 2028 will likely trigger (E9). Route (a) is fastest and reinforces the IBM-partner posture.

### Phase 0 — Stabilise & Listen (Weeks 0–4)

1. **Protect the beachhead.** Commission an internal health check of the NS&I adviser portal: incidents, version currency, support SLA performance. It must be flawless — it is the entire evidence base for our story. Any outage becomes "Backbase is part of the problem."
2. **Stakeholder re-map.** Post-resignation, the map has changed: Sir Jim Harra (CEO), interim/new CIO and Transformation Director, HM Treasury sponsor team, NS&I NEDs, Infrastructure & Projects Authority reviewers, IBM UK account leadership. Identify who owns the PAC-mandated re-plan.
3. **Listening exercise, explicitly no-pitch.** Senior Backbase exec requests a relationship review: "We want to understand where we fell short and what you need from us as an incumbent supplier during the reset." Output: validated root cause (H1–H4) and permission to contribute to the re-plan.

### Phase 1 — Reframe the Argument (Weeks 4–10)

4. **Produce an outside-in "Digital Experience Recovery Assessment"** (this repo's Ignite Assess pipeline, public sources + anything NS&I shares). Core content:
   - Conservative, evidenced comparison of *complete-the-custom-build* vs *extend-the-proven-platform* for the digital front end: cost-to-complete, time-to-first-customer-value, delivery risk, exit/lock-in risk.
   - Anchored to the PAC's own recommendations — realistic bottom-up plan, transparent milestones, risk framework — so adopting Backbase *is* compliance with Parliament, not a vendor pivot.
   - Value-for-money language throughout (Accounting Officer test, Managing Public Money), not vendor ROI language.
5. **Quantify with a defensible ROI model** (roi-hypothesis-builder → roi-financial-modeler): benefit streams = avoided build cost, earlier cost-to-serve reduction (the programme's original objective), reduced consultancy burn (PAC flagged £43m), and risk-adjusted delay cost. All assumptions explicit, sensitivity-tested at ±25–50%.

### Phase 2 — Build the Coalition (Weeks 8–16)

6. **IBM alliance conversation** at partner-leadership level (not via the NS&I account first): offer the "product layer" construct. IBM's incentive: the NAO named them; delivering something visible in 2027 on a productised platform rehabilitates the account they are contractually tied to until 2028.
7. **Treasury-facing narrative** via NS&I sponsors: a one-page value-for-money case a Treasury official could repeat — "reuse the platform already live at NS&I; first customer-facing release in months, not years."
8. **Decision gate (Day ~90):** If IBM engages → pursue Route (a), contract change control. If IBM stonewalls → prepare for Route (c), the post-2028 re-procurement, and build the bid case now.

### Phase 3 — Land a Visible Quick Win (Months 4–9)

9. **Target the bereavement journey.** The £476m / 37,500-family scandal (E8) is NS&I's most painful public wound and is process/digital in nature (their search process failed to find all products of a deceased customer). A bounded, high-visibility **digital bereavement claims journey** — case tracking, proactive estate contact, single product view — is politically resonant, humanly meaningful, and deliverable on the existing Backbase estate without waiting for the big replatform decision.
10. **Expand from the adviser portal outward** once the quick win lands: retail web servicing on the same platform, then mobile — matching exactly the scope IBM has not delivered (E6: "no timetable for replacing its website").
