## Tim's direction (the meat)

1. **The exercise will sell.** He's living the 35-person version: GTM OS collapsed his marketing structure (content dissolved, product marketing collapsing, MLT/cadence impact); he sketches org charts with Claude and validates with ex-CMOs. For a bank the problem is 10-100x (cross-functional, power dynamics, regulation, separation of concern, unit economics). "You'll call McKinsey… or maybe ask Backbase." Needs **assets that show the end-state**: "the piece of paper you bring to your next executive: this is how we're going to reorg." Could become a VC tool.
2. **Flip it into demand gen.** The big pivot: make it the **Org Chart Builder** — self-serve, instant, like the conversational-banking builder. orgchart.backbase.com: see your org chart, build models, three flavors on the spot from our benchmarking. Don't gate behind sales conversion; give access. Funnel logic: **Builder = awareness at scale (free) · workshop/engagement = mid-funnel conversion · paid org-design service = the new deliverable.**
3. **The credibility recipe from the digital wave:** (a) market-leading product that delivered the promise; (b) *be what you sell* — Backbase was digital-native running agile when Spotify/ING made it hot; customers "hanging on your lips"; explained concepts, didn't prescribe; (c) relentless public thought leadership until the phone rings. Today: noisier; OpenAI/Anthropic/Mistral/Microsoft/McKinsey already in the boardroom; "we need more access." Jouk must be out there Vercel-CEO-style (open-weights act, Kimi K3 zero-retention, shows his work); brand/.com lift in 4-8 weeks.
4. **Commercial model warning:** the €350K-entry + consumption enterprise motion "already lost half the battle." AI-native competition comes **outcome-based, full stop** (Sierra raised $1B, buying the market). Backbase will have to move outcome-based within **3-6 months** or lose the agentic conversations. "You don't buy IP anymore; you lease it, rent it; you want the outcome."
5. **Buyer questions, not internal buckets.** The digital/agentic product split is internal clarity; "the buyer doesn't buy in two buckets." Frame everything against real buyer questions: "How do I bring my digital channels to an AI-native world? What stays deterministic, what becomes reasoning-level, and how do I do it safely?"
6. **Where to apply first: the agentic banking GTM** — closest to a full claim (A&A on rails with Casisto), dedicated product leader, **weekly go-to-market cadence** ("every week a sledgehammer"). Tim is unblocking backbase.com (off Webflow) to ship daily.

8. **The system, not the hero:** answer to Shyam's "is this egotistical?" — no: build the shared rails (GTM OS = our Banking OS).
