# Field Chief Digital Officer — Job Description

## Role Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Field Chief Digital Officer (Field CDO) |
| **Location** | United States (remote, with travel to client sites and Backbase offices) |
| **Status** | Pilot role — US market, with intent to scale globally |
| **Reports To** | VP Value Consulting / Head of Field Strategy |
| **Works With** | Value Consulting, Sales (AEs), Product Marketing, Solution Engineering, Partners |

## Why This Role Exists

Banks making platform decisions want to hear from someone who has sat in their chair. Not a vendor pitch. Not a consultant framework. A peer who has faced the same technology decisions, navigated the same vendor landscape, managed the same board dynamics, and come out the other side with lessons worth sharing.

Today, Backbase has:
- **Sales teams** with deep product knowledge but limited credibility in a bank boardroom discussing core modernization trade-offs
- **Value Consultants** with rigorous methodology but who enter the picture after the relationship is established

The gap is the first conversation — the one where a banking executive decides whether Backbase is worth their time. The Field CDO fills that gap. They are a former banking CDO who speaks the language of banking technology decisions, carries the scars of real platform migrations, and can translate Backbase's value into terms that resonate with a CTO staring at a 15-year-old FIS implementation.

The five responsibilities are not independent — they form a reinforcing loop mapped to the sales lifecycle:

**The key transitions:**
- **Thought Leadership → Warm Account Activation**: Published POVs and speaking engagements create inbound interest. Prospects recognize the CDO's name before the first call. The CDO's network already trusts the perspective.
- **Warm Account Activation → Engagement**: Once a prospect's challenge is identified and qualified, the CDO brings in Value Consulting for an Ignite Inspire engagement. The CDO participates as executive sponsor and peer voice — they do not hand off and disappear.
- **Engagement → Product & Innovation Advisory**: During and after every engagement, the CDO feeds AI use case signals back to Product. Which agents did prospects ask about? Which AI capabilities did they challenge? What are they building internally for $2-3M that Backbase could offer for $300-500K? This intelligence shapes the AI product roadmap and sharpens positioning for the next conversation.
- **Innovation / Engagement → EverBank**: Success stories from EverBank and other accounts feed the thought leadership engine. Expansion opportunities within EverBank create new engagement cycles.
- **EverBank → Thought Leadership**: Reference stories ("here's what we did at EverBank") are the most credible form of thought leadership. The cycle restarts.

### 1. Thought Leadership & Market Presence (15%)

The Field CDO's thought leadership creates the air cover that makes every other responsibility more effective:

- **Executive conversations**: Walk into board-level and C-suite discussions at prospect banks with a point of view on their technology landscape. Not selling — sharing perspective. The kind of conversation that happens between CDOs at an industry dinner, except this one leads somewhere.
- **Published thought leadership**: Authored perspectives on core banking modernization, digital engagement architecture, fintech partnership strategy, and AI in banking operations. Published through Backbase channels, LinkedIn, industry publications, and speaking engagements.
- **Competitive positioning**: Articulate why a Backbase-centered digital engagement layer is a better strategic choice than FIS Digital One (core-replacement play), Temenos (analyst-positioned), or Fiserv (infrastructure-led). This is not feature comparison — it is strategic framing from someone who has evaluated these options from the buy side.
- **Industry presence**: Conference speaking, executive roundtables, advisory board participation, analyst briefings where a practitioner voice carries more weight than a vendor voice.

**Key reference:** The competitive analysis framework in the Strategy Workshop ([agent-1-strategy.md](../Ignite%20Inspire/agent-1-strategy.md)) and the [FIS D1 Vs Backbase Battlecard](../FIS%20D1%20Vs%20Backbase%20Battlecard%20updated%20June%202025.pdf) provide the competitive intelligence backbone. The Field CDO adds experiential credibility on top.

### 2. Warm Account Activation (35%)

The Field CDO's financial services network is a strategic asset — and activating it is the single largest responsibility. This is not cold lead generation. It is the systematic warming, activation, and qualification of accounts through peer relationships.

- **Network-driven activation**: Identify and reach out to banking executives from prior relationships. These are warm conversations, not cold calls — the CDO is calling a former colleague, a conference contact, a board peer. The goal is to re-engage dormant relationships and create new ones through referrals.
- **Account warming**: For target accounts where the CDO has no direct relationship, build credibility through thought leadership, mutual connections, and industry events before making the first approach. Every touchpoint warms the account.
- **First-level qualification**: Determine whether a prospect's challenges align with Backbase's value proposition. Use the [Discovery Question Bank](../discovery_question_bank.md) as a conversational framework, not a checklist.
- **Transition to engagement**: When a conversation shows promise, bring in Value Consulting for an Ignite Inspire engagement. The CDO stays involved as executive sponsor and peer voice throughout the engagement — they do not hand off and disappear.
- **Market intelligence**: Feed back what the market is saying — what technology decisions are being made, what pain points are top of mind, which competitors are showing up in evaluations. This intelligence shapes Backbase's positioning.

**Success looks like**: A banking CTO takes the CDO's call because they trust the perspective, not because they're interested in a vendor demo. The conversation naturally surfaces a challenge that Backbase can address. The CDO transitions the conversation into an Ignite Inspire engagement where they continue to participate.

### 3. Engagement Facilitation (35%)

The Field CDO is not a bystander during engagements — they are an active participant who adds strategic weight and executive credibility throughout the consulting phase:

- **Ignite Inspire workshops**: Participate in Strategy Alignment sessions (Agent 1) and Executive Readouts where C-level credibility matters most. The CDO speaks from experience: "When I was at [bank], we faced the same decision. Here's what we considered."
- **Discovery facilitation**: Contribute to discovery sessions using the hypothesis-driven approach from the [Value Consulting methodology](../methodologies/engagement_types.md). The CDO's banking experience helps form sharper hypotheses and ask better questions.
- **Executive air cover**: During multi-day engagements, maintain the executive relationship while consultants focus on methodology. Ensure the C-suite stays engaged and the engagement stays strategically aligned.

**Boundary**: The Field CDO participates in assessment and consulting phases but is NOT the primary owner of proposal building, ROI modeling, or roadmap construction. Those responsibilities remain with Value Consulting. The CDO's involvement tapers after the discovery/assessment phase unless the engagement benefits from continued executive sponsorship.

**Key references:** [Ignite Inspire Consultant Guide](../Ignite%20Inspire/CONSULTANT_GUIDE.md), [Engagement Types](../methodologies/engagement_types.md)

### 4. Product & Innovation Advisory (10%)

Backbase has executed a fundamental product pivot — from an engagement banking platform to an **AI-native Banking Operating System**. This is the single biggest positioning shift in the company's history. The platform now includes a semantic intelligence layer (NEXUS), pre-built AI agents for retail/wealth/commercial banking, an Agent Studio for custom agent development, and agentic process automation that handles routine cases autonomously.

The Field CDO is the market-facing voice of this pivot. Having sat on the buy side, they know which AI capabilities matter and which are noise. They have heard every AI pitch from every vendor. They know the difference between a slide deck and a production deployment.

- **AI use case translation**: Banks are drowning in AI vendor pitches. The CDO cuts through the noise with practitioner credibility. Which AI agents create real value? Where does agentic automation actually work in banking operations? The CDO translates Backbase's three-pillar AI framework (Embedded AI, Conversational Engine, Process Automation) into language a banking CTO trusts — grounded in real use cases, not product demos.

  Key use cases the CDO should be able to articulate from experience:
  - **Retail**: Smart product recommendations, digital onboarding automation, dispute triage agents, fraud alert triage, SAR narrative generation
  - **Wealth**: Review prep copilots (4-6 hours → <1 hour), next-best-action engines, compliance screening agents, suitability assessment automation
  - **Commercial**: RM briefing copilots (2 hours → 15 minutes), credit memo drafting (6 hours → 1 hour), KYB automation (5 days → same day), trade finance document checking

- **Product-market alignment gatekeeper**: When Backbase launches new AI capabilities, the CDO pressure-tests them against market reality. Is this the right capability for the US market? Would a CTO actually prioritize this over the 15 other AI initiatives competing for budget? The CDO aligns Sales, Product Marketing, and Value Consulting on positioning — which segments care, which pain points it solves, and how to frame it in an executive conversation.

- **The "build vs. buy" conversation**: The CDO's most powerful AI conversation is the three-layer transparency argument: raw LLM compute costs $5-15K/year, but building the banking guardrails, agent orchestration, PII filtering, compliance controls, and pre-built agents costs $2-3M/year. Backbase provides all of that for $300-500K/year — 80-85% cheaper than DIY, with a 6-month head start. This is a conversation only a former CDO can credibly lead, because they have been the one signing the $2-3M checks.

- **Competitive AI positioning**: Microsoft Copilot is horizontal ($30/user/month) — it does not understand KYC, suitability, or regulatory compliance. Generic AI consultancies (Deloitte, Accenture) advise on AI strategy but do not build production agents. Core vendors (FIS, Fiserv) are bolting AI onto legacy architectures. The CDO articulates why a purpose-built, banking-native AI layer is structurally different.

- **Use case development feedback loop**: Every executive conversation surfaces new AI use cases that banks are trying to solve. The CDO feeds these back to Product — not as feature requests, but as validated market signals with context: which segments want this, how much they would pay, and how it competes with what they are building internally.

### 5. Strategic Client Partnering — EverBank (5%)

The Field CDO serves as Strategic Client Partner (SCP) for EverBank:

- **Relationship ownership**: Senior executive relationship across the EverBank account. Regular strategic touchpoints, not just project check-ins.
- **Expansion identification**: Spot opportunities for additional Backbase initiatives (new domains, new journeys, platform extensions) based on understanding EverBank's strategic direction.
- **Success storytelling**: With EverBank's permission, translate their Backbase journey into reference material for prospects. A CDO saying "I've seen this work at a bank like yours" is the most powerful sales tool available.
- **Quarterly business reviews**: Lead or co-lead QBRs with an executive lens, ensuring the engagement stays tied to business outcomes, not just project milestones.

## Required Qualifications

- **10+ years in financial services**, with progressive leadership in technology, digital, or innovation roles
- **3+ years as CDO, CTO, SVP Digital, or equivalent** at a US bank or financial institution
- **Direct experience with platform technology decisions**: core banking evaluations, digital channel platform selections, middleware modernization, or similar enterprise-scale decisions
- **Working familiarity with US banking tech stacks**: FIS (Modern Banking Platform, Digital One, Code Connect), Fiserv (DNA, Signature, Premier), Jack Henry (Symitar, SilverLake), Temenos (Transact, Infinity), and the middleware/integration layers around them
- **Executive presence and communication**: Comfortable in a boardroom. Can hold a strategic conversation with a bank CEO without slides.
- **Existing banking executive network**: Relationships that can be activated for warm introductions within the first 90 days
- **Willingness to travel**: 40-60% travel to client sites, conferences, and Backbase offices

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience leading or sponsoring a digital banking platform implementation
- Exposure to both retail and commercial/business banking technology
- Published thought leadership (articles, conference talks, advisory board roles)
- Familiarity with open banking / Banking-as-a-Service architectures
- Experience with fintech partnership evaluation and incubation

## What This Role Is NOT

To prevent role drift, be explicit about boundaries:

- **Not a sales rep**: The CDO does not own quota, negotiate contracts, or manage deal mechanics. They generate and qualify leads, then partner with Sales.
- **Not a full-time consultant**: The CDO participates in engagements but does not own deliverable production (ROI models, assessment reports, roadmaps). That is Value Consulting's domain.
- **Not a product marketer**: The CDO creates thought leadership from experience, not product collateral. Product Marketing supports with assets and amplification.
- **Not an account manager**: Even as SCP for EverBank, the CDO focuses on strategic direction, not operational account management (renewals, support escalations, project management).
