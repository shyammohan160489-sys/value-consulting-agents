# Colby Mangers — Field CDO Onboarding Plan

## Onboarding Philosophy

This is not standard consultant onboarding.

Colby comes with 10+ years of banking leadership, executive presence, technology evaluation experience, and an existing industry network. He does not need domain training, communication coaching, or product deep-dives at the feature level.

What he needs:
1. **Backbase product fluency** — enough to credibly discuss the platform and AI-native positioning in executive conversations
2. **Value Consulting methodology** — how Backbase structures engagements, so he can participate in Ignite Inspire workshops
3. **Competitive positioning** — the specific arguments against FIS D1, Temenos, Fiserv, and consultancies
4. **AI-native narrative** — Backbase's three-pillar AI framework, build vs. buy economics, and domain-specific AI use cases
5. **Internal navigation** — who does what, how to work with Sales, VC, Product Marketing, and Partners

**Design principle**: Minimize time to first external conversation. Every week from Week 3 onward includes market-facing activity. Colby's value depreciates in training; it appreciates the moment he is in front of a banking executive.

---

## How This Differs from Standard VC Onboarding

| Dimension | Standard VC (13 weeks) | Colby / Field CDO (8 weeks) |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Domain training (banking, FS) | Extensive (2-3 weeks) | **Skipped** — he brings this |
| Executive communication | Included | **Skipped** — he brings this |
| Product training | Deep (demo-capable) | **Strategic-level** — architecture, AI-native positioning, not UI |
| VC methodology | Full curriculum | **Selective** — participation in Ignite, not deliverable production |
| Client-facing activity | Week 6-8 | **Week 3** — network outreach begins |
| Deliverable production | Core competency | **Not a focus** — Colby participates, does not produce |
| Warm account activation | Not part of role | **Primary activity** from Week 3 |
| Thought leadership | Not part of role | **Active** from Week 2 |
| AI-native product advisory | Not part of role | **Week 2 onward** — learns the AI pivot, becomes market voice |

---

## The Onboarding Structure

The onboarding is organized around **four phases** mapped to the deal cycle — not around what Colby learns, but around what he does:

| Phase | Weeks | Deal Cycle Stage | What Colby Does |
|-------|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **1. Foundation** | 1-2 | — | Learns Backbase, builds his narrative, maps his network |
| **2. Activation** | 3-4 | Awareness → Qualify | First outreach, first engagement shadow, first thought leadership |
| **3. Engagement** | 5-6 | Qualify → Engage | Co-leads engagements, runs prospect meetings, publishes consistently |
| **4. Independence** | 7-8 | Full cycle | Operates the deal cycle end-to-end. 60-day review. |
