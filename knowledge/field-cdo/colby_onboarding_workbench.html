The Three Ignite Types

Colby needs to understand all three and be capable of running Inspire and Assess. Ops is being co-created.

IGNITE INSPIRE
Quick inspiration for qualified leads
Light pre-work, senior exec audience. "Let's inspire you with what's possible." Pre-work → 4 workshops → Innovation Day → output presentation.
Colby's role: Primary KPI — generate these AND run them
Cortex pipeline: Agents 0-6 (engagement plan → workshops → use cases → presentation)
IGNITE ASSESS
Detailed value roadmap & business case
Client invests more time. Journey assessment, capability maturity, ROI modeling, roadmap. Full VC framework.
Colby's role: Participates in discovery, assessment, presentation — not deal advisory
Cortex pipeline: Discovery → Capability → ROI → Roadmap → Assembly agents
IGNITE OPS
AI / Agentic Process Automation wedge
Where is the AI use case appetite? Explore AI agents, APA, and process automation opportunities for prospects or existing customers.
Colby's role: Spots opportunities during activation + helps co-create the framework
Cortex pipeline: Being refined — AI agent catalogs + AI pricing playbook as foundation
DEAL ADVISORY (Awareness Only)
Pricing, scenario analysis, commercial structuring
What happens after assessment. Colby should understand this exists but it's not his responsibility today. May evolve.
Colby's role: Awareness only — review examples, understand the downstream
Examples: Schroders commercial, CIH proposal, Danske pricing
