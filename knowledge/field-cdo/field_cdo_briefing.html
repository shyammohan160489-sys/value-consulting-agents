THE AI-NATIVE PIVOT

The CDO is the market-facing voice of Backbase’s AI pivot

Backbase has pivoted from engagement banking platform to AI-native Banking OS. The CDO translates this to the C-suite.

THE CDO’S AI CONVERSATIONS
Use case translation
Banks are drowning in AI vendor pitches. The CDO cuts through noise with practitioner credibility — which AI agents create real value, where agentic automation works, where it’s hype.
Build vs. buy framing
Raw LLM compute: $5-15K/yr. Building banking guardrails, agent orchestration, PII filtering: $2-3M/yr. Backbase: $300-500K/yr. Only a former CDO who signed $2-3M checks can credibly make this case.
Product-market gatekeeper
When Backbase ships new AI capabilities, the CDO pressure-tests against market reality. Right capability for right segment? Aligns Sales, PMM, and VC on positioning.
AI USE CASES THE CDO LEADS WITH
Retail
Onboarding automation, dispute triage agents, fraud alert triage, smart recommendations
Wealth
Review prep copilot (4-6hrs → <1hr), compliance screening, suitability assessment agents
Commercial
RM briefing copilot (2hrs → 15min), credit memo drafting, KYB automation (5 days → same day)
COMPETITIVE POSITIONING
vs. Microsoft Copilot: horizontal, not banking-native. vs. Core vendors: AI bolted onto legacy. vs. Consultancies: advise but don’t build. vs. DIY: 80-85% cheaper with 6-month head start.

COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

How the Field CDO changes how Backbase competes

Peer credibility enables conversations that vendor positioning cannot.

vs. FIS Digital One
FIS says: stay with us, just upgrade. D1 is a core replacement disguised as modernization.
“I was on FIS. Here’s why decoupling your digital layer gives you options a single-vendor stack never will.”
vs. Temenos
Leads with Gartner/Forrester positioning and European references. Limited US practitioner credibility.
“Analyst rankings measure vendor capability, not your outcome. Let me tell you what actually matters for a US bank.”
vs. Fiserv
Infrastructure company. Owns the plumbing. Digital offering is an extension of infrastructure revenue.
“Your digital experience should be independent of your plumbing decisions. Here’s how to think about that separation.”
vs. Consultancies
Deloitte, Accenture, McKinsey advise. They don’t build. 200-page reports, no outcomes.
“We’re not here to write a report. We’re here to prove the value and build it.”
