# Field CDO — Team & Capability Building Plan

## Executive Summary

The Field CDO is launching as a one-person pilot in the US. This document defines the path from pilot validation to a global practice of 8-12 Field CDOs across four regions. Each phase has clear success criteria, decision gates, and investment requirements.

The model is deliberately conservative: prove first, then replicate. No region launches without a validated playbook from the previous phase.

---

## Phase 1: Pilot Validation (Months 1-6)

### Structure

```
Field CDO (US Pilot)
├── Reports to: VP Value Consulting / Head of Field Strategy
├── SCP for: EverBank
├── Territory: US market (all segments)
└── Support from: VC, Sales, Product Marketing, SE (existing headcount)
```

One person. No dedicated team. The pilot uses existing Backbase resources and validates whether the model works before any incremental investment.

### Support Requirements

The Field CDO does not work alone — they leverage existing teams:

| Function | Support Provided | Effort (% of existing resource) |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| Value Consulting | Methodology training, engagement co-facilitation, quality assurance on CDO's engagement contributions | 15-20% of one VC lead's time for Months 1-3, then 5-10% |
| Sales (AE) | Pipeline coordination, co-selling on early prospects, CRM hygiene | 10% of AE time in CDO's target accounts |
| Product Marketing | Thought leadership editing and amplification, competitive intelligence updates, event coordination | 10-15% of one PMM's time |
| Solution Engineering | Platform architecture sessions during onboarding, on-call for technical credibility in prospect conversations | 5% of one SE's time |
| Marketing (Demand Gen) | Content distribution, social amplification, event logistics | 5% of existing demand gen capacity |
| Executive Sponsor | Strategic direction, obstacle removal, stakeholder alignment | 2-3 hours / month |

### Tools and Knowledge Required

| Resource | Source | Purpose |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Cortex repository | This repo | Value Consulting methodology, domain knowledge, engagement playbooks |
| Backbase MCP Infobank | [MCP Integration Guide](../platforms/backbase-mcp-integration.md) | Product capability queries during prospect conversations and engagement prep |
| Discovery Question Bank | [discovery_question_bank.md](../discovery_question_bank.md) | Conversational frameworks for prospect and client interactions |
| Competitive Intelligence | [FIS D1 Battlecard](../FIS%20D1%20Vs%20Backbase%20Battlecard%20updated%20June%202025.pdf) + market updates | Positioning in executive conversations |
| CRM | Salesforce (existing) | Pipeline tracking, contact management, activity logging |
| Content Platform | LinkedIn + Backbase blog (existing) | Thought leadership publication |

### 6-Month Decision Gate

At Month 6, the pilot is evaluated against these criteria:

| Criterion | Minimum for "Continue" | Stretch for "Accelerate" |
|-----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Executive conversations generated | 8+ unique banking executives engaged | 15+ with 3+ at $20B+ institutions |
| Leads progressed to Ignite | 2+ conversations converted to Ignite Inspire engagement | 4+ with at least 1 in contracting |
| Thought leadership published | 3+ pieces with measurable engagement | 6+ pieces, 1+ speaking engagement, growing audience |
| EverBank account health | Maintained or improved; 1+ expansion opportunity identified | Active expansion initiative underway |
| Internal collaboration rating | Sales, VC, PMM rate collaboration as "effective" | Rated as "transformative" — changed how they approach accounts |
| Pipeline value influenced | $500K+ in attributed pipeline | $1.5M+ with clear CDO attribution |

**Decision options:**
- **Accelerate**: Criteria exceeded. Move to Phase 2 hiring immediately.
- **Continue**: Minimum criteria met. Extend pilot 3 months with specific improvement targets.
- **Pivot**: Criteria not met. Analyze root cause. Options: redefine role scope, reassign territory, or wind down.

### Shared Capabilities to Build

During Phase 2, the team codifies what was learned in Phase 1:

| Capability | Description | Owner | Timeline |
|-----------|-------------|-------|----------|

| **Executive Roundtable Program** | Quarterly events (dinner, virtual session) where CDOs host 8-12 banking executives for peer discussion. Not a Backbase sales event — a genuine peer forum with Backbase as convener. | CDO team + Marketing | First event Month 10-12 |
| **Innovation Advisory Toolkit** | Structured framework for fintech partnership evaluation, innovation pipeline assessment, PoC design. Based on CDOs' banking experience. Potential standalone offering. | CDO team | Month 12-15 |
| **Competitive Intelligence Feed** | Monthly update integrating CDO market intelligence with PMM competitive analysis. What CDOs are hearing in the market + what PMM is tracking from analysts and competitors. | Practice Coordinator + PMM | Month 8 onward |
| **Ignite Facilitation Guide for CDOs** | Adapted version of the [Consultant Guide](../Ignite%20Inspire/CONSULTANT_GUIDE.md) focused on the CDO's role: where to participate, what to contribute, where to defer to VC. Not the full methodology — the CDO's entry points. | CDO #1 + VC Lead | Month 7-9 |

## Phase 3: Global Replication (Months 18-36)

### Regional Adaptation

Each region has distinct banking market dynamics. The Field CDO model adapts while maintaining the core playbook:

| Region | Market Context | CDO Profile Needed | Key Adaptations |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **EMEA** | Open banking (PSD2/PSD3), pan-European banking groups, strong regulatory overlay, UK as distinct sub-market | Former CDO/CTO from European bank, ideally with cross-border experience. UK may need its own CDO. | Thought leadership leans on regulatory drivers and open banking. Competitive set includes Temenos (stronger in EU), Finastra. Executive roundtables align with Money20/20 Europe, EBAday, Sibos. |
| **APAC** | Mobile-first markets (SE Asia), super-app dynamics, variable regulatory maturity, distinct sub-markets (ANZ, SEA, India) | Former CDO from APAC bank or fintech with regional breadth. Language and cultural fluency critical. | Digital engagement framing shifts from "modernize legacy" to "compete with super-apps." Innovation advisory more prominent (fintech ecosystem is more dynamic). |
| **LATAM** | Rapid digital adoption, neobank competition (Nubank, Ualá), open finance mandates (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia), concentrated banking markets | Former CDO from LATAM bank, ideally with experience in Brazil or Mexico. Must navigate Spanish/Portuguese. | Neobank competitive pressure is the entry point, not legacy modernization. CDO credibility in fast-moving digital markets matters more than legacy experience. |

### Regional Launch Model

Each region follows the same pattern:

```
Month 0-1:  Identify and hire regional Field CDO
Month 1-2:  Onboarding (adapted from US onboarding plan, localized)
Month 2-4:  Network activation and first market conversations
Month 4-6:  First Ignite engagement with CDO involvement
Month 6:    Regional decision gate (same criteria as US Phase 1)
Month 7-12: Second CDO hire if gate passes
```

### Global Practice Coordination

```
Global Head of Field Strategy
├── US Practice (3-4 CDOs + Practice Coordinator)
├── EMEA Practice (1-2 CDOs)
├── APAC Practice (1-2 CDOs)
├── LATAM Practice (1 CDO)
└── Global Practice Operations
    ├── Shared playbook and methodology
    ├── Global thought leadership calendar
    ├── Cross-regional executive events (Sibos, Money20/20)
    └── Market intelligence aggregation
```

**Global coordination cadence:**
- Weekly: CDO Slack channel — market intelligence, win/loss sharing, competitive signals
- Monthly: Global CDO call — pipeline review, playbook updates, cross-regional opportunities
- Quarterly: Practice review — KPIs, investment decisions, playbook evolution
- Annually: CDO Summit — in-person, strategy alignment, relationship building

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Role becomes "just sales"** — CDO drifts into quota-carrying, transactional selling | Medium | High — destroys the peer credibility that makes the role work | KPI design separates thought leadership and engagement facilitation from deal closure. CDO never carries quota. Regular role calibration with executive sponsor. |
| **CDO resists methodology** — experienced banker dismisses VC frameworks as "consultant stuff" | Medium | Medium — CDO operates independently but disconnects from Backbase's differentiated approach | Differentiated onboarding (see [Onboarding Plan](onboarding_plan.md)) respects banking experience while demonstrating methodology value. CDO participates in Ignite, not runs it. |
| **Talent scarcity** — hard to find banking CDOs willing to join a platform vendor | Medium | High — can't scale the practice | Position as "practice builder," not "vendor sales." Emphasize thought leadership, peer community, and career evolution. Compensation reflects seniority. Advisory / fractional models as alternative. |
| **Network exhaustion** — CDO's existing contacts run out | Low | Medium — pipeline dries up after initial network is activated | Executive roundtable program replenishes network. Thought leadership attracts inbound. CDO community shares contacts across regions. |
| **Pilot fails** — CDO doesn't generate pipeline | Low-Medium | Medium — investment lost, model unproven | 6-month decision gate limits exposure. Root cause analysis before abandoning model (was it the person, the market, or the model?). Pivot options: redefine scope, reassign territory, or try with a different profile. |
| **Competitive response** — FIS/Temenos/Fiserv copy the model | Low | Low — the model is people-dependent and hard to replicate quickly | First-mover advantage. Relationships compound. Playbook and practice infrastructure are defensible. |
