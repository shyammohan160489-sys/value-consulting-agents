## Context

The Field CDO bridges a structural gap in Backbase's go-to-market: sales teams have product knowledge but limited banking credibility at the C-level; consulting teams have methodology but limited executive networks. The Field CDO is a former banking CDO who brings peer-level credibility to executive conversations and converts thought leadership into qualified pipeline.

**Pilot:** US market, starting March 2026. First hire is SCP for EverBank.
