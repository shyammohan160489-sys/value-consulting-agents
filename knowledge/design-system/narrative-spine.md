## 1. Positioning (the through-line)

- **The thesis (lead with this):** *"The next decade will not be defined by better channels, but by better **operating models**."* Retire "better channels / better experience" as the headline — lead with the operating model.
- **What Backbase is:** *"The **AI-Native Banking OS** that unifies your frontline."* A **banking operating system** — not a portal, not a point solution, not "engagement banking" (**retire that legacy phrase**).
- **Banking OS = the Control Plane** of the unified frontline — *"the system to coordinate and govern the work across your frontline,"* between customers, employees, agents and hundreds of downstream systems. Add-on-top, **no rip-and-replace**. Where credible, name the layers: **Nexus** (the shared source of truth / "Palantir of banking") and **Sentinel** (governed, auditable execution).
- **The core idea — the Unified Frontline:** digital channels, front office, and operations; customers, employees, and AI agents — all running on **"one shared truth and one execution engine, with explicit authorization for every action."** The bank runs **as one**.
- **The product surface:** 2 domains (Customer Engagement · Banking Operations) → 4 solutions — **Digital Banking · Conversational Banking · Relationship Intelligence · Customer Operations**. Map every opportunity to these. (Full detail: [`banking-os.md`](../product/banking-os.md).)
- **Mission line (use in closes):** *"Put bankers back in the driver's seat — fully equipped to lead the AI revolution and unlock remarkable growth and efficiency."*
- **Trust proof:** *"Trusted by 120+ leading banks."*

## 2. The signature narrative device — From → To

Open every narrative on the pain, resolve to the unified state. This is the spine's most portable asset — it works for a cold pitch, a workshop, an assessment, or a negotiation deck.

- **FROM** — *"frontline fragmentation — a losing formula."* No shared truth, no shared outcomes.
  > "6–12 systems per journey. 30–60% manual work. High cost-to-serve. Slow cycles. AI stuck in pilots. **~60% of the bank's work lives in the whitespace *between* systems** — the handoffs, exceptions and manual coordination."
- **TO** — *"a Unified Frontline that runs the bank as one."*
  > "Digital channels, front office, and operations. Customers, employees, and AI agents. Running on one shared truth and one execution engine — governed and auditable."
- **The value of crossing over (three pools):** cost-to-serve **↓ 20–40%** · conversion / cross-sell / retention **↑ 10–25%** · AI approval-to-production **3–5× faster** → **$150M–$300M+** annual value unlock (bank-size dependent).

Map every engagement's *own* discovery findings onto this FROM→TO scaffold. The client's specific fragmentation becomes the FROM; the Unified Frontline becomes the TO.

## 3. The opinionated stance (the wedge)

Use this to differentiate — it reframes the whole AI conversation in our favour:

> **"AI won't fix fragmentation. Without shared context and clear rules, it amplifies it."**

AI bolted onto a fragmented frontline makes the mess faster and more expensive. You need the **OS underneath first** — shared truth, an execution engine, explicit authorization. That is what Backbase is. This is the argument that turns "we're already doing AI" into "you're automating chaos."

## 5. Tone

Direct, opinionated, problem-first. **Short declarative sentences.** Name a technical term once, then move on — keep it CEO-level. Quantify the pain ("crosses twelve systems"). State the stance, don't hedge it. Outcomes before features, always (cf. the SparD gold-standard look: the hero shows investment / returns / payback / time-to-value, not capabilities).

## 7. How each pipeline applies the spine

| Pipeline / skill | Apply the spine by… |
|---|---|
| Discovery / transcript interpreter | Cluster findings into the **FROM** picture — name the client's specific fragmentation, handoffs, exceptions |
| Ignite workshops (strategy/member/employee/arch) | Use **FROM→TO** as the workshop arc; the stance (§3) as the AI-readiness framing |
| Capability / value assessment | Maturity = distance from a Unified Frontline; gaps = where the frontline isn't "running as one" |
| Journey builder | Map current journeys as fragmented swimlanes (FROM) → unified, orchestrated future state (TO) |
| ROI / business case | Outcomes-first hero; tie value levers to "one execution engine" efficiency and lifecycle ownership |
| Narrative assembler · executive-briefing · frontline-long-form · frontline-slides | Open on FROM→TO, anchor on AI-Native Banking OS, close on the mission line; dark/architecture visual language |
| Prototype | Show the Unified Frontline literally — one shared truth, web→mobile continuity, agents + audit trail |
| Deal-desk / negotiation | The Banking OS is *what they're buying*; price defends the OS, not a feature list |

**Applies in all directions** — cold pitches (SEB, Pictet, …), competitive displacements, and live negotiations alike. The spine is engagement-type-agnostic.
