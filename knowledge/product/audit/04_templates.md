# Banking OS Alignment Audit — Slice 04: `templates/**`

**Auditor:** Banking OS Alignment Checker
**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Standard:** `knowledge/product/banking-os.md` (v2026.06) + `knowledge/design-system/narrative-spine.md` (v2026.06)
**Scope:** 24 files under `templates/**` (output templates, long-form, presentations, ROI/questionnaire, inputs, emails).

## Method & headline finding

Grepped `templates/` for `engagement banking`, `better channels`, `better experience`, `digital experience platform`, `omnichannel`, `engagement platform` — **zero literal hits**. The legacy positioning phrase has already been scrubbed from the literal copy. The real risk in this slice is **structural**: the two flagship narrative/exec templates (`assessment_report.md`, `executive_summary.md`) and the ROI template do **not carry the From→To frame, the operating-model thesis, the 4-solutions map, or the three value pools** as scaffolding — so a consultant filling them in inherits a generic "platform / unified frontline" story rather than the canonical Banking OS spine. Two templates also still hard-code **pre-2026 product names** (`Grand Central iPaaS`, `HELIX / Customer Brain`, `Flow Foundation`, `Digital Engage / Digital Assist`) that contradict `banking-os.md` (which names Banking OS, Nexus, Sentinel, and the 4 solutions).

## Cascade recommendation

**Fix `templates/outputs/assessment_report.md` first — it is the single highest-leverage change in this slice.** It is the mandated structure for the flagship Ignite Assess deliverable (the template explicitly forbids omitting any of its 7 sections), it is the most heavily client-facing template in `templates/`, and it carries **two of the three HIGH findings at once**: (1) the narrative skeleton sells a 2024-era "unified banking platform / progressive modernization" story instead of the Banking OS operating-model thesis with a real From→To act and the 4-solutions map, and (2) its lifecycle value model hard-codes deprecated product names (Grand Central, Digital Engage, Digital Assist). Reframing this one file — open on FROM (frontline fragmentation), resolve to TO (Unified Frontline on one shared truth + one execution engine), anchor on Banking OS = control plane / Nexus / Sentinel, map opportunities to the 4 solutions, and recast the value-leakage funnels as Customer Resolution Loops quantified against the three value pools — cascades the canonical spine into every assessment readout the team produces, and the same edits become the pattern to apply to `executive_summary.md`, `roi_report.md`, and the `capability_assessment.md` glossary.
