Conversational Banking  •  Customers
turns natural language into governed banking services and fast resolutions — for customers and employees.
Capability | Short description / example
Assist | Helps customers ask questions, find information, and navigate digital banking. Example: “How do I increase my card limit?” or “Where can I find my statements?”
Transact | Lets customers complete routine authenticated actions through conversation. Example: freeze a card, download a statement, update contact details, book an appointment, or check payment status.
Resolve | Turn customer needs into governed resolution loops that execute faster across cases, workflows, fulfilment, and status updates. Example: start a dispute, report a missing payment, submit a complaint, provide missing onboarding documents, or track the progress of a service request (linked with Customer Operations solutions).
Grow | Identify timely moments where the bank can help customers make financial progress while deepening the relationship. Example: help a customer complete a savings goal, improve cash-flow visibility, finish an application, prepare for a life event, reduce avoidable fees, or consider a relevant next-best action — without positioning it as formal advice (linked with Relationship Intelligence solutions).
