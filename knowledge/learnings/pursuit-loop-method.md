# The Pursuit Loop — 24-hour deal acceleration method

**What it is:** the operating method that emerged on the BACB pre-RFP pursuit (Jul 2026), generalisable to any fast-moving deal. The premise: in a compressed procurement (weeks, not months), **momentum is strategy** — the vendor who metabolises intel fastest and returns it as value-shaped artifacts writes the client's decision documents. This file codifies the loop so any consultant (or agent) can run it.

## The loop (each turn < 24h)

1. **Ingest instantly.** Every artifact — transcript, AI meeting summary, Slack thread, client deck — lands in `Engagement/[client]/Input/` with a digest the same day. Transcription garbles are flagged, never silently trusted (e.g. "Terminox"→Temenos, "Sienna"→?). Discrepancies between what the team *says* and what public filings *show* are logged as verify-items, not resolved by assumption (BACB: 195 vs "500–600" staff; "no parent" vs 87.8% Libyan Foreign Bank).
2. **Convert meetings into instruments.** Before any client touch: a **question bank** — every question carries "→ what it tells us" so the team knows why it's asked. Two variants: live-interview form (probing, sensitive) and written form (client-safe, 1–2-day turnaround). **Hold-back discipline:** questions that die on paper (lost-client stories, what-stalled-last-time, unstated fail criteria) are explicitly reserved for the room.
3. **Build the value hypothesis BEFORE discovery.** Triangulate from three sources — repo benchmarks, public filings (annual report, registries), and stated facts from calls — into a value-chain × lever skeleton where **every number is tagged: KNOWN (source) / ASSUMED (benchmark, confidence) / ASK (questionnaire line)**. The questionnaire is then designed to fill exactly the ASK cells. Result: a draft business case exists before anyone grants discovery time — "Ignite by questionnaire."
4. **Sell POV-first.** The entry artifact is never a capability deck — it's a point of view that plays the client's own plan back, challenged (roadmap rewire). Their language is absorbed and returned ("concentration risk", "future-proofing"); their objectives are affirmed before their phasing is attacked.
5. **Draft independent, then compare.** When a colleague owns a parallel artifact (e.g. the demo agenda), the advisor drafts their version BLIND first, then reconciles. Prevents anchoring; surfaces genuine disagreements instead of polite convergence.
6. **Stress-test your own thesis.** Every attack line ships with its falsifier: what evidence would flip it, and what the pivot would be (BACB: if clients want visibility over transacting, wave-1 messaging pivots but adopt-don't-build survives; if staff count is ~200 not ~600, the case re-anchors from cost to retention/revenue). Never rely on yes.
7. **Correct the record at source.** Misreads (e.g. "CXO phase = Ignite accepted" when it was post-signature) are fixed in the memory/deal-state file the moment they're caught — a wrong fact that survives two sessions becomes the team's truth.
8. **Everything feeds the next artifact.** Digest → question bank → questionnaire answers → value hypothesis → demo script → leave-behind → RFP response. No artifact is terminal; each is an input contract for the next.

## Why it wins

- **The business case beats the demo.** Demos get you down-selected; the client's *internal approval memo* gets you bought. Whoever supplies the CxO-ready case writes it in their own frame.
- **Speed compounds trust.** Same-day digests and next-day artifacts read as "this is what they'll be like to work with."
- **Challenge earns the partner seat.** Clients running vendor refreshes (BACB, Cumberland) explicitly score "will they lead us" — agreement doesn't demonstrate leadership.

## Reuse checklist (start of any fast pursuit)

- [ ] Input/ + digest discipline live from day one
- [ ] Question bank with "what it tells us" per question; live vs written split; hold-backs marked
- [ ] Public-filings sweep (assets, income, staff, ownership, strategy) with verify-flags vs team hearsay
- [ ] Value-chain × lever skeleton: KNOWN / ASSUMED / ASK tagging; questionnaire designed off the ASK column
- [ ] POV deck that rewires their roadmap (not a capability deck)
- [ ] Stress-test section: each thesis + falsifier + pivot
- [ ] Deal-state memory updated every session; corrections applied at source
